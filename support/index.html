Common questions

The app says my pages won't fit under my limit.
PDFyes won't shrink images so far that small text becomes unreadable. Choose a larger limit, or remove a few pages; the app tells you which pages take up the most space.

A photo couldn't be added.
If the photo is stored only in iCloud, connect to the internet once so your iPhone can download it, then try again.

How is 1 MB counted?
PDFyes counts 1 MB as 1,000,000 bytes, the strictest way upload forms measure, so your file fits everywhere.

Are my documents uploaded anywhere?
No. Everything happens on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.
