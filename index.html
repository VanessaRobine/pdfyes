PDFyes

One PDF that fits your upload limit.

PDFyes for iPhone combines up to 30 photos or screenshots into a single PDF, and keeps it under the size limit you choose: 1, 2, 5 or 10 MB, or your own.

  • Reorder, rotate or remove pages
  • Clean up dim, shadowy photos of documents
  • Preview and zoom in to check small text before you share
  • Save to Files or share anywhere

🔒 Everything happens on your iPhone. No account, no uploads, no tracking.
