Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

PDFyes does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Your photos stay on your iPhone

PDFyes reads only the photos you choose and creates the PDF entirely on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

No account

PDFyes doesn't ask you to sign up or sign in.

No tracking or analytics

PDFyes contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking tools.

No location data in your PDFs

Location and camera information stored in your photos is not copied into the PDF.

Temporary files

While you work, PDFyes keeps temporary copies of your selected photos in the app's private storage. They are deleted automatically the next time the app starts.

Sharing is up to you

A PDF leaves your iPhone only when you choose to save or share it.
