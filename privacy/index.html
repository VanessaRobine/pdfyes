Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Your photos, scans, files and PDFs never leave your iPhone unless you choose to save or share them. PDFyes has no account and shows no ads.

Your photos stay on your iPhone

PDFyes reads only the photos, scans and files you choose and creates the PDF entirely on your device. Nothing you add to a PDF is uploaded to any server.

No account

PDFyes doesn't ask you to sign up or sign in.

Measuring our ads

We advertise PDFyes on Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and TikTok. To know whether those ads work, PDFyes includes the Meta SDK and the TikTok Business SDK in a measurement-only setup. When you install and open the app, these tools may send Meta and TikTok basic technical information, such as device model, operating system version, app version, IP address and the fact that the app was installed or opened. This lets them count how many installs came from an ad. Under Apple's definition this counts as tracking, which is why the App Store page says so.

  • The first time you open PDFyes, Apple asks whether to allow tracking. Only if you tap Allow are Meta and TikTok given your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to measure our ads. If you tap Ask App Not to Track, they never receive it. You can change this anytime in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • No custom in-app events are sent, and none of your photos, files, PDFs or file names are ever shared.
  • Ad attribution uses Apple's SKAdNetwork, which reports installs in aggregate without identifying you.
  • Meta and TikTok handle this data under their own privacy policies: Meta and TikTok for Business.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. PDFyes never sees your payment details.

No location data in your PDFs

Location and camera information stored in your photos is not copied into the PDF.

Temporary files

While you work, PDFyes keeps temporary copies of your selected photos in the app's private storage. They are deleted automatically the next time the app starts.

Sharing is up to you

A PDF leaves your iPhone only when you choose to save or share it.
